# NOIRADIX Studio & PixelArt v2.2.6

## Editing & Tooling

- Transform the free-selection tool into a full-fidelity Freehand Selection Brush across NOIRADIX Studio and PixelArt.
- Instant real-time visual feedback: On pointerdown (single pixel click) and pointermove (brush strokes), every selected pixel lights up immediately with a bright indigo/purple fill (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.45)`) and a crisp `#a5b4fc` inner border on the canvas overlay.
- Free selection is no longer restricted to pre-painted pixels; artists can freely brush or click any coordinates within the canvas bounds.
- Multi-stroke accumulation: Holding `Ctrl`/`Cmd` keeps previous selection strokes illuminated and accumulates multiple clicks and strokes into one unified selection mask upon releasing the modifier.
- Full selection operation support: Move, Cut (`Ctrl+X`), Copy (`Ctrl+C`), Delete (`Del`), and Recolor accurately preserve the freehand selection mask.
- Cursor update: Hovering with the freehand selection tool displays a crosshair cursor and brush outline. Pressing `Escape` cleanly cancels pending selection gestures.
