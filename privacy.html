Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026 • Effective Date: November 14, 2026
Project files stay local by default: Editing and PIX2 parsing take place in your browser. The app does not automatically upload your artwork to a NOIRADIX account or project server. Browser storage, explicitly selected files, the website host, and optional third-party resources are described below.

1. Information and App Data

NOIRADIX does not provide user accounts, advertising profiles, analytics, or session-replay features. The application does not automatically transmit your PIX2 projects or artwork to a NOIRADIX backend. When you visit the hosted website, its hosting and network providers may process routine request data such as your IP address, browser details, requested page, and request time under their own policies.

  • Project content: PIX2 files, artwork, palettes, and animation data are processed in the browser. They are written to disk only through a save or export action you initiate, or stored in browser storage for draft recovery.
  • Information you share: If you copy a PIX2 prompt or project context and submit it to an AI provider, or follow a link to GitHub, Discord, or another service, that provider receives information according to your action and its own privacy terms. NOIRADIX does not send the content on your behalf.
  • Cookies and analytics: The NOIRADIX application does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not include an analytics or session-replay service. Hosting providers and third-party resources may have their own technical logging or storage practices.

2. Local Storage and Browser Technologies

NOIRADIX Studio uses browser storage on the current site origin for app settings, open-workspace state, file-handle references, and recovery drafts. Browser storage is tied to that browser profile and site; it is not a cloud backup.

  • localStorage: Stores interface preferences, workspace and tab metadata, and draft or recovery content needed to restore work.
  • IndexedDB: Stores workspace data and, where supported, references to file or directory handles you selected. A browser may require you to grant access again after a reload or according to its permission policy.
  • Retention and clearing: These entries remain in the browser profile until the app removes them or you clear site data. Clearing browser data, using private browsing, browser storage limits, or device failure may remove drafts; save important work to a file you control.

3. Local File System Access API

When supported by your browser, file pickers let you select files or directories to open, import, or save. The browser mediates access and may ask for permission before reading or writing. Access is limited to the selected items and granted permissions; support and permission persistence vary by browser. You can instead use ordinary file open, download, and export flows where available.

4. Third-Party Resources and Links

Some pages may load resources hosted by third parties, such as icon stylesheets, profile images, or listing badges, and may link to external services. Loading an external resource can disclose request information, including your IP address and browser details, to that resource's provider. External sites and providers have their own privacy policies and are not controlled by NOIRADIX. The editor itself does not send your project data through these image or stylesheet requests.

5. Children's Privacy

NOIRADIX does not provide accounts or ask users to submit personal information through the editor. The website is not designed to establish a user's age. Parents or guardians with a privacy question can contact the maintainer using the links below.

6. Open Source Transparency

The application source is published on GitHub at github.com/dotlinux26/noiradix-studio. Project components may have different licenses; see the repository's license notices for details.
